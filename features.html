01. Syllabus Upload

Upload your syllabus. Your semester is set up before you finish your coffee.

Drop in a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or Excel file, a photo, or pasted text. Class times, every assignment and exam, your professor’s contact details, office hours, the attendance rule, and the late policy land where they belong, ready to review and edit. What used to take an evening of retyping takes a minute, and the original file stays in the class page.

02. Import from Canvas

Already in Canvas? Bring the whole semester over in one paste.

Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, and Moodle each give you a private calendar link. Paste it once and every due date lands in Semester HQ, sorted into the right class. It stays connected: the next time you open the app it re-reads the feed, so a deadline your professor moves moves here too, and nothing is ever added twice. Lectures, office hours, and last term’s work stay out of it.

03. Class Pages

Everything about a class on one page, not buried on page four of a PDF.

Every class gets its own page: what is due next, the next exam, class times, notes, and flashcards. The syllabus details live there too, with a one-tap email to your professor, the next office hours, the TA, and a private absence tracker that tells you exactly where you stand with the attendance rule.

app.semester-hq.com
04. Clubs & Teams

Your club’s calendar, in every member’s planner.

For clubs, sports teams, and sororities and fraternities. Officers post meetings, practices, games, and dues deadlines once, and they show up on every member’s calendar next to their classes. Announcements land on members’ dashboards instead of getting buried in a group chat, and officers can see who is coming and who has not answered. An attendance grid shows who keeps turning up across the term, and exports the roster with every RSVP as a spreadsheet. The GroupMe, the Instagram, and the link dues get sent to sit under the club’s name, so nobody has to ask for them again.

One person uploads the syllabus. The whole section gets every deadline.

They get the class times, every assignment and exam date, and the office hours in one tap, and when a deadline moves, everyone’s copy follows. Only the schedule is shared. 06. Study Groups

Stop losing three days in the group chat just to pick a time.

Everyone drags to mark when they are free, and Semester HQ shows the time that works for the most people. It can draft your side for you: one tap fills in every hour you are not in class. Schedule it in one click and it lands on everyone’s calendar, or make it weekly and book the rest of the term at once. Open a session to see who is going, who is a maybe, and who has not answered yet. Split tasks with owners and due dates, grab an unclaimed one with a single tap, share notes and flashcard decks, and keep the conversation in the group’s own chat.

07. Quick Add

Type it the way you’d text yourself.

“bio lab report fri 5pm” becomes a lab for BIO 201, due Friday at 5:00 PM. Semester HQ picks out the class, the day, the time, and what kind of work it is while you type, and shows you exactly what it understood before you hit enter. Add an exclamation point and it is high priority.

app.semester-hq.com
08. Notebook

A page that already knows what kind of notes you are taking.

Notes are filed by class, with highlights, checklists, PDF export, and search across everything you have written. Start a lecture on the Cornell layout and the page splits into a cue column, ruled notes, and a summary band; when you study, one tap covers the notes so you answer from your own cues. Start a lab on the report layout and you get the eight sections your TA is grading, a data table that grows a row at a time, and a tracker showing what you still have not written.

The rest of the term

Calendar

Class meetings, deadlines, exams, study sessions, and club events on one calendar, color-coded by class. Month, week, day, and year views, and you can drag anything to a new day to move it.

Exam Prep

Every exam gets a live countdown and its own prep page: a topic checklist you rate not yet, shaky, or got it, and study sessions spread out on your calendar around your classes.

Projects

Start from a template (research paper, group presentation, lab report, case study, portfolio, capstone) and the milestones are spaced out between today and the due date, with tasks, links, and notes together.

Flashcards

Build decks by class or turn a note into cards. Spaced repetition brings each card back right before you would forget it, so ten minutes a day beats a three-hour cram.

Heads Up

The bell in the corner gathers what is overdue, due today and tomorrow, exams closing in, sessions, milestones, and cards ready for review. Semester HQ sends no notifications at all: no permission prompt, nothing on your lock screen.
