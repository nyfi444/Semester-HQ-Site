What actually happens when you upload one

Drop in a PDF, a photo of a printed syllabus, or pasted text. Semester HQ reads it and pulls out the course details, the assignment schedule, and every exam date it can find, then lays them into your planner ready to edit. Nothing is locked in: if it gets a date wrong or misses something, you fix it in a few seconds, the same as you would with anything you typed by hand.

What used to take an evening of manual entry — typing every due date into a calendar, setting up the course, guessing at what the grading breakdown actually means for your workload — takes a minute instead. And it's not marketed as some flashy AI feature: it's just what happens when you upload a syllabus, the way it should have worked the whole time.

Questions

About the syllabus planner.

Does it work with any syllabus format?

Yes. Upload a PDF, take a photo of a printed syllabus, or paste in the text directly. Semester HQ reads whichever format you give it and pulls out the same details either way.

What if it gets something wrong?

Everything it adds is fully editable before it's final. Nothing is locked in, so you can fix a wrong date or add something it missed in a few seconds.

Is my syllabus stored anywhere after I upload it?

No. The file is sent to Anthropic's AI, read once to extract the details, then discarded. It's never stored afterward and never used to train any model.
