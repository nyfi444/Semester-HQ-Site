For clubs, teams, and chapters

Officers post meetings, practices, games, philanthropy events, and dues deadlines once, and they land on every member's own calendar. Announcements show up on members' dashboards instead of getting lost in a group chat, and officers see who's coming and who hasn't RSVPed for required events.

Share a class once and every student gets the class times, every deadline, and the office hours in one tap, and updates follow automatically. Students also get study groups with a find-a-time heatmap, RSVPs, shared tasks, notes, and flashcards.
