For clubs, teams, and chapters

Officers post meetings, practices, games, philanthropy events, and dues deadlines once, and they land on every member's own calendar. Announcements show up on members' dashboards instead of getting lost in a group chat, and officers see who's coming and who hasn't RSVPed for required events. An attendance grid tracks who turns up across the term and exports the roster with every RSVP as a spreadsheet, and the GroupMe, Instagram, and dues link sit under the club's name where members can find them.

For classes and departments

Share a class once and every student gets the class times, every deadline, and the office hours in one tap, and updates follow automatically. Students also get study groups with a find-a-time heatmap, weekly sessions, RSVPs, shared tasks, notes, and flashcards, and can pull their own deadlines in from Canvas or whichever LMS your campus runs.

What every member gets

Their own full Semester HQ planner: calendar, syllabus upload, Canvas and LMS imports, class pages, exam prep, projects, flashcards, and more.

Who this is for

Student clubs and orgs, sports and club teams, sororities and fraternities, academic departments, professors covering a course, tutoring centers, or anyone buying more than one seat.
