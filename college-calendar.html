Questions

About the college calendar.

Does it show my recurring classes automatically?

Yes. Add a course with its meeting days and times once, and every class session appears on your calendar for the rest of the semester without re-entering it.

Can I see the whole year, not just one month?

Yes, there's a year view alongside month, week, and day, so you can see the whole semester's shape at a glance or zoom into one day's schedule.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in your phone's browser with no app download needed, and every view works the same on a laptop, phone, or library computer.
