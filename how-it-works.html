How it works

Three steps to a semester
that is already set up.

Most planners die in week three because the setup is an evening of retyping. Semester HQ starts from the one document every professor already gave you.

  1. Step one

    Upload the syllabus

    Drop in the PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or Excel file, a photo of a printed copy, or pasted text. Class times, every assignment and exam, the professor’s contact details, office hours, the attendance limit, and the late policy come out in about a minute, ready to check and edit. The original file stays in the class page. Already have it all in Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, or Moodle? Paste your calendar link instead and every due date comes across at once.

    14 deadlines2 examsOffice hours3 absences allowed
  2. Step two

    See the semester

    Everything lands on one calendar and on one page per class: what is due next, the next exam with a countdown, the next office hours, and a private tally of your absences against the limit. Anything the syllabus did not cover, you add by typing it the way you would text it.

  3. Step three

    Share it with your people

    Share a class so your whole section gets every deadline in one tap. Find the hour your study group is actually free. Let a club officer post practice once and have it land on every member’s calendar, next to their classes.
