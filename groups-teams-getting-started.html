For groups & teams

Run your club, team, or study group from one calendar.

Officers post the events. Members answer Going or Can’t, and every event lands on their own Semester HQ calendar next to their classes. This guide covers setting up, inviting members, roles, attendance, and when a group plan makes sense.

Get started → Try the live demo

What a club or team gets

Clubs & Teams gives your club, team, chapter, or organization one calendar that officers run and every member gets. Officers post events and announcements. Members answer Going or Can’t, and each event lands on their own Semester HQ calendar, next to their classes.

Every club has a calendar, announcements, a chat, shared files, and a members list, plus an Admin tab only officers see. Pick a color for the club and add up to six quick links under its name, such as your GroupMe, Instagram, or where dues go. The club shares its own events, files, and chat. Each member’s personal planner stays theirs.

How to get your group running in six steps

The first four take a few minutes. The last two are what keep the group organized all term.

  1. Create the club or team

    Open Clubs & Teams and choose Start a club or team. Give it a name, say what it is (a club, a team, a sorority or fraternity, or an organization), pick a color, and add your title, such as President or Captain. You become the founder and an officer.

  2. Invite members

    Share the invite link, or read out the 6-character code. Members choose Join with code and type it in. When they join, each person picks general body or adds their position.

  3. Add the calendar and events

    On the Calendar tab, add each event with a type (meeting, practice, game or match, social, service or philanthropy, or deadline or dues), a date, a time, a place, and notes. Turn on weekly repeat for practices and set how many weeks. Mark the events that are required. Change a weekly event later and you can update the ones after it too.

  4. Set roles

    Officers add events, post announcements, share files, and see who is coming. Members answer events, chat, and edit their own name and title. Only the founder makes someone an officer, and when a new member adds a position like Treasurer, the founder is asked whether that person should get officer access.

  5. Run attendance

    The Admin tab shows an attendance grid: one row per member, one column for each of the last six events and the next two, and who said Going or Can’t to each. Officers also see which required events still have members who have not answered. Export the roster with every event’s answers as a CSV for the sheet you already keep.

  6. Move to a group plan when the group is ready

    Each member needs Semester HQ Plus at $7.99 a month to join, or a seat on a group plan. A group plan covers everyone for $5.99 per member per month, self-serve for 5 to 50 seats at app.semester-hq.com/group-admin.html. Buy it, share one invite link, and each member takes a seat. For more than 50 members, or for an invoice, a PO, or a W-9, use the quote form on the group pricing page.

Study groups: finding a time that works

A study group belongs to a class. Start one from Study Groups and share the link or code. Everyone marks their free hours on a shared grid, and the grid darkens where more people overlap. One tap drafts your free hours from your class schedule.

Schedule a session with a place and it appears on every member’s calendar with Going, Maybe, and Can’t. Make it weekly for up to 15 weeks. Split the prep into tasks with owners and due dates, share notes, flashcard decks, and files, and keep the conversation in the group’s chat. Each group gets its own color. The study group scheduler guide walks through it step by step.

Questions about clubs, teams, and groups

Does every member need their own subscription?

Each member needs Semester HQ Plus at $7.99 a month, or a seat on a group plan. A group plan covers members for $5.99 each per month, self-serve for 5 to 50 seats.

Who can post events and announcements?

Officers. Whoever starts the club is the founder and an officer, and only the founder can make someone else an officer.

Can I see who came to an event?

You can see who said they would come. The attendance grid on the Admin tab shows each member’s Going or Can’t for recent and upcoming events, and it exports as a CSV. It records what members answered, not a check-in at the door.

Does it remind members before an event?

No. Semester HQ sends no notifications. Events sit on each member’s calendar and in their Heads up panel, and required events that still need an answer are flagged on the club’s page.

What if our group has more than 50 members?

Self-serve group plans cover 5 to 50 seats. For more than 50, or if you need an invoice, a PO, or a W-9, use the quote form on semester-hq.com/group-pricing.html.
