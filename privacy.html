Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy explains what Semester HQ ("we," "us") collects when you use the planner, who can see it, and what we do with it. We built this to be as boring and predictable as possible. We're not in the business of your data.

The short version

  • You can try Semester HQ without an account. Nothing you enter in the demo leaves your browser or gets saved: it's gone once you close or reload the tab.
  • With an account, your planner is stored in our database so it syncs across your devices. It's private to you unless you choose to share something.
  • When you share with other people (a study group, a shared class, or a club or team), the people with that group's code can see what's shared there, including your display name. Your progress, notes, and absences are never shared.
  • If you turn on reminders, we store your device's push subscription and your upcoming reminder schedule so we can notify you when the app is closed.
  • When you use a feature that reads a document, photo, or pasted text (like syllabus upload or quick capture), that content is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to pull out the details. We don't keep a copy of the file.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't use it to train AI models, and there are no ads or ad trackers in the planner.

What we collect

Account info. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo through Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with an email link, we receive your email address. You can set a display name in Settings.

Your planner. What you create in Semester HQ: classes and meeting times, assignments and to-dos, calendar time blocks, notes and attachments, flashcards and review history, study timer sessions, projects, exam topics and study plans, applications you're tracking, syllabus details (like office hours and course policies), the absences you log, and your settings. With an account, this is stored in our database (Google Firebase) so it syncs across your devices, and a copy is kept in your browser on each signed-in device so the app works offline. Files you attach may be stored in Firebase Storage. Your absence log, progress, and notes are private to you.

Things you share with other people. Semester HQ has three ways to plan with others. In each, a 6-character code (or an invite link containing it) is how people join, and anyone signed in who has the code can view the group's basic information.

  • Study groups. Members can see the group's name and member list (display names and chosen colors), the weekly availability each member adds, study sessions and RSVPs, shared tasks, chat messages, and notes, flashcard decks, files, and links that members share into the group.
  • Shared classes. When you share a class, anyone with its code can see the course name and code, instructor, location, class meeting times, the titles, types, and due dates of its assignments and exams, the syllabus details you've added (such as the professor's contact information, office hours, TAs, and course policies), the school, section, and term, and your display name as the person who shared it. If you let classmates find it by searching, other signed-in students can find it by school and course code. When classmates join, we record that they joined so you can see how many have. Your completion status, notes, files, and absences are never part of a shared class.
  • Clubs & Teams. Members can see the club's name, description, and member list (display names, officer roles, and titles), events (including dates, times, locations, and details), announcements and who posted them, and each member's RSVPs. Officers use RSVPs to see who's attending.
  • Group plans. If someone covers Semester HQ for you through a group plan, that plan's admins can see your name, the email address you signed in with, and the date you took a seat, so they know who they're paying for. They cannot see anything inside your planner. If you buy a group plan, we process your payment through Stripe the same way as any other subscription.

Only share what you're comfortable with the other members seeing. Don't post other people's private information.

Reminders and push notifications. If you turn on reminders, your browser creates a push subscription (a device-specific address and encryption keys), which we store with your time zone and a schedule of your reminders for the next 10 days: for example, the names and due times of assignments, exams, study sessions, and club events. Our server uses it to send notifications through your browser's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla, or Microsoft, depending on your device). Notification content is encrypted so the push service can't read it. Turning reminders off in Settings removes this device's subscription.

Documents, photos, and text you send to our AI features. These features send content through our server to Anthropic's Claude API to extract details:

  • Syllabus and assignment upload (PDFs, photos, or pasted text)
  • Quick capture (a photo of a whiteboard or assignment sheet, a PDF, or text, including files shared into the app from your phone)
  • Pasting a job, internship, or scholarship posting into the applications tracker
  • Planning a project, pulling exam topics from a study guide, and making flashcards from a note

We don't retain a copy of what you send after the request completes. What's extracted is shown to you to review, and whatever you choose to keep is saved in your planner like anything else. On Android, a file you share into Semester HQ is held briefly in your browser's storage on your device until the app opens it.

Payment info. When you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details directly. We receive your email and your subscription status (active, renewed, past due, or canceled), which we use to unlock or lock your account.

Crash reports, diagnostics, and security. If something in the app or on this site fails, it sends a report so we can fix it: the error message, the feature and page it happened on, your browser type and screen size, the app version, whether you were signed in or offline, and the last few screens you opened and requests the app made just before. Reports never include your planner content, file names, or email address, and are deleted after 30 days. Our server uses your IP address briefly to limit repeated requests and protect against abuse.

Messages you send us. If you use our contact or group pricing form, we receive what you enter (like your name, email, and message) and get an email notification so we can reply.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't use your uploaded documents or planner content to train AI models.
  • We don't run third-party advertising or ad trackers inside the planner.
  • We don't share your progress, notes, or absences with classmates, group members, or club officers.
  • We don't track grades or GPA.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers required to run Semester HQ, each receiving only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, and file storage
  • Cloudflare: our backend server, which handles AI requests, reminders, payments, and forms
  • GitHub: hosting for the website and app files
  • Anthropic: reading documents, photos, and text when you use an AI feature
  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla, or Microsoft push services: delivering notifications to your device, if you turn reminders on
  • Resend and Google Workspace: emailing us your form submissions and handling our email

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Our marketing website (semester-hq.com, not the planner app) uses Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate traffic, and records anonymous counts of clicks on buttons like "Try the Demo" and "Get Started." Google Analytics uses cookies. None of this is used for advertising, and none of it can see anything you do inside the planner.

Your controls

  • Export: Settings → Data → Export backup downloads your planner as a JSON file, any time.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Settings → Reminders, or block notifications in your browser or device settings.
  • Sharing: stop sharing a class, leave a study group or club, or delete your own messages and shared items at any time. Group owners and club officers can remove members and content in their groups.
  • Recently Deleted: items you delete are recoverable for 30 days before being permanently removed.
  • Erase this device: Settings → Data → Erase all data wipes the copy on that device.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account & Sync → Delete account cancels your subscription and permanently deletes your planner, notes, reminder schedule and push subscriptions, and your sign-in. It doesn't automatically remove things you've shared with other people, like messages in a study group, a class you shared, or your membership in a club. Leave those or stop sharing first, or email us and we'll remove them for you. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.
  • Other requests: to access, correct, or delete your information, email us.

Children's privacy

Semester HQ is intended for students aged 13 and older, and everyone confirms they're at least 13 when signing in. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and, for material changes, let you know in the app.
