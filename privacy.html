Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

This policy covers the Semester HQ planner at app.semester-hq.com and this website. It explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean Semester HQ.

What we collect

Account info. Your email address and the password you choose, or, if you use Google, your name, email address, and profile photo. Firebase Authentication (Google) handles sign-in. We never see your password.

Your planner. What you put in Semester HQ: classes and meeting times, assignments, to-dos, notes, files you attach, flashcards, study sessions, projects, exam topics, applications you track, syllabus details, the absences you log, any calendar feed link you connect, and your settings. The demo, used without an account, saves nothing.

What you share with others. People join a study group, shared class, or club with a 6-character code or an invite link. Members can see what is shared there and your display name. In a study group, that is the availability you add, sessions and RSVPs, tasks, chat, and anything members share in. In a shared class, it is the class details, meeting times, and deadline titles and dates. In a club or team, it is the member list with titles, events, announcements, chat, files, and RSVPs; officers can export the roster and RSVPs. If a group plan pays for you, its admins see your name, email, and when you took a seat. Your progress, notes, and absences are never shared.

Documents and text you ask us to read. Syllabus upload, quick capture, pasting a job or scholarship posting, and making exam topics or flashcards from your notes send that content through our server to Anthropic’s Claude API, which pulls out the details for you to review. Anthropic does not train on it. A syllabus you upload stays in your class page until you delete it or your account.

Calendar feeds. If you paste a Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle, or other calendar link, we store the link with your planner and our server fetches it to read your due dates. We never write back to your school’s system, and we do not keep a copy of the calendar.

Payment info. Stripe collects your card details directly. We receive your email and whether your subscription is active.

Diagnostics and security. When something breaks, the app sends an error report: the error, the page, your browser type and screen size, and the app version. Reports never include your planner content or email. Our server uses your IP address briefly to limit repeated requests.

Messages you send us. What you enter in our contact or group pricing form.

How we use it

  • To run your planner and sync it across your devices.
  • To bill your subscription.
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share it for advertising, we run no ads, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Semester HQ, each limited to what it needs:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in (including Google sign-in), database, and file storage.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Cloudflare: our server, bot checks on our forms and sign-up (Turnstile), and cookieless traffic counts on this website (Web Analytics).
  • Anthropic: reading documents and text you choose to upload.
  • GitHub: hosting for the website and app files.
  • Resend and Google Workspace: delivering form submissions to us and handling our email.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare: the site’s typefaces, which your browser loads from them.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies. The planner stores a copy of your data and your sign-in session in your browser’s own storage so it opens fast and works offline. Signing out clears it. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages, and Turnstile may set one on Cloudflare’s challenge domain. This website also counts clicks on buttons like “Get started” with no cookies and no per-person identity.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can access, correct, export, and delete your data. Export any time from Settings → Data. Delete your account from Settings → Account & Sync, which cancels your subscription and deletes your planner, files, and sign-in. For anything else, email hello@semester-hq.com. We reply within 45 days and confirm it’s you by asking you to write from your account email. You may use an authorized agent. We never treat you differently for using a right.

California residents (CCPA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We collect identifiers (name, email, account id), the content you create, subscription status, and error reports. We get it from you, from Google if you use Google sign-in, and from Stripe when you pay. We use it only for the purposes above and disclose it only to the providers listed above. We do not sell or share personal information, including that of anyone under 16, and have not in the past 12 months, so there is nothing to opt out of. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

The controller is Semester HQ, LLC, 9660 Falls of Neuse Road, Suite 138 #273, Raleigh, NC 27615, USA (hello@semester-hq.com). We rely on our contract with you to run your account and billing, on legitimate interests for security and error reports, and on your consent when you upload a document or connect a calendar feed. You can withdraw consent by deleting the file or removing the feed. Your data is processed in the United States under standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You also have the rights to restrict and object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children under 13

Semester HQ is for people 13 and older, and everyone confirms their age when they create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database rules let only your account read your planner. Card details never reach our servers. No security is perfect, so use a strong password or keep your Google account secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. For material changes, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@semester-hq.com
