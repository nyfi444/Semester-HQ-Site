Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains what Semester HQ ("we," "us") collects when you use the planner, who can see it, and what we do with it. We built this to be as boring and predictable as possible. We're not in the business of your data.

The short version

  • You can try Semester HQ without an account. Nothing you enter in the demo leaves your browser or gets saved: it's gone once you close or reload the tab.
  • With an account, your planner is stored in our database so it syncs across your devices. It's private to you unless you choose to share something.
  • When you share with other people (a study group, a shared class, or a club or team), the people with that group's code can see what's shared there, including your display name. Your progress, notes, and absences are never shared.
  • Semester HQ never sends notifications. There is no push subscription, no browser permission prompt, and nothing about your schedule is stored anywhere in order to alert you.
  • When you use a feature that reads a document, photo, or pasted text (like syllabus upload or quick capture), that content is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to pull out the details. Anthropic doesn't train on it and doesn't keep it beyond its API data retention policy. A syllabus you upload stays in your class page, so it can be re-read, until you delete the file or your account.
  • If you connect a Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, or Moodle calendar feed, we store that link in your planner and our server fetches it to read your due dates. We only ever read it, we never write anything back to your LMS, and you can disconnect it at any time.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't use it to train AI models, and there are no ads or ad trackers in the planner.

What we collect

Account info. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo through Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with an email link, we receive your email address. You can set a display name in Settings.

Your planner. What you create in Semester HQ: classes and meeting times, assignments and to-dos, calendar time blocks, notes and attachments, flashcards and review history, study timer sessions, projects, exam topics and study plans, applications you're tracking, syllabus details (like office hours and course policies), the absences you log, any calendar feed link you connect, and your settings. With an account, this is stored in our database (Google Firebase) so it syncs across your devices, and a copy is kept in your browser on each signed-in device so the app works offline. Files you attach may be stored in Firebase Storage. Your absence log, progress, and notes are private to you.

Things you share with other people. Semester HQ has three ways to plan with others. In each, a 6-character code (or an invite link containing it) is how people join, and anyone signed in who has the code can view the group's basic information.

  • Study groups. Members can see the group's name and member list (display names and chosen colors), the weekly availability each member adds, study sessions and RSVPs, shared tasks, chat messages, and notes, flashcard decks, files, and links that members share into the group.
  • Shared classes. When you share a class, anyone with its code can see the course name and code, instructor, location, class meeting times, the titles, types, and due dates of its assignments and exams, the syllabus details you've added (such as the professor's contact information, office hours, TAs, and course policies), the school, section, and term, and your display name as the person who shared it. If you let classmates find it by searching, other signed-in students can find it by school and course code. When classmates join, we record that they joined so you can see how many have. Your completion status, notes, files, and absences are never part of a shared class.
  • Clubs & Teams. Members can see the club's name, description, any links its officers add (such as a group chat, a social account, or where dues are sent), the member list (display names, officer roles, and titles), events (including dates, times, locations, and details), announcements and who posted them, and each member's RSVPs. Officers use RSVPs to see who's attending, including an attendance view across past and upcoming events, and can export the roster and those RSVPs as a spreadsheet.
  • Group plans. If someone covers Semester HQ for you through a group plan, that plan's admins can see your name, the email address you signed in with, and the date you took a seat, so they know who they're paying for. They cannot see anything inside your planner. If you buy a group plan, we process your payment through Stripe the same way as any other subscription.

Only share what you're comfortable with the other members seeing. Don't post other people's private information.

Notifications. Semester HQ doesn't send any. We never ask your browser for notification permission, never create a push subscription, and never upload a schedule of what's coming up. Nothing about your deadlines is copied anywhere to make an alert possible. What needs your attention is shown inside the app, behind the bell, and only when you open it. Earlier versions of Semester HQ did offer reminders; if you used them, that subscription and schedule are deleted, and deleting your account removes any remaining copy.

Documents, photos, and text you send to our AI features. These features send content through our server to Anthropic's Claude API to extract details:

  • Syllabus and assignment upload (PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Excel files, photos including iPhone HEIC, or pasted text)
  • Quick capture (a photo of a whiteboard or assignment sheet, a PDF, or text, including files shared into the app from your phone)
  • Pasting a job, internship, or scholarship posting into the applications tracker
  • Planning a project, pulling exam topics from a study guide, and making flashcards from a note

Anthropic reads what you send to extract the details. It doesn't use it to train models, and it doesn't keep it beyond its API data retention policy. A syllabus you upload also stays in your class page (stored in Firebase Storage) so it can be re-read later, until you delete the file or your account. What's extracted is shown to you to review, and whatever you choose to keep is saved in your planner like anything else. On Android, a file you share into Semester HQ is held briefly in your browser's storage on your device until the app opens it.

Calendar feeds you connect. If you import from Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle, or any other calendar, you give us the feed link your school issued you. That link is stored with your planner, and it is treated as private, because for most systems the link itself is what grants access to it. Our server (Cloudflare) fetches the link on your behalf, because your browser is not allowed to read it directly, and returns the calendar to your device, where your due dates are matched to your classes. We request it over a secure connection only, we do not store a copy of the calendar on our server, and we never send anything to your LMS or change anything in it. The feed is fetched when you first connect it and again while you have Semester HQ open, so that moved due dates follow. You can remove a feed at any time in Settings, which deletes the link; the assignments it already created stay in your planner until you delete them. If you would rather not connect anything, you can upload an exported calendar file instead, which is read entirely on your device.

Payment info. When you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details directly. We receive your email and your subscription status (active, renewed, past due, or canceled), which we use to unlock or lock your account.

Crash reports, diagnostics, and security. If something in the app or on this site fails, it sends a report so we can fix it: the error message, the feature and page it happened on, your browser type and screen size, the app version, whether you were signed in or offline, and the last few screens you opened and requests the app made just before. Reports never include your planner content, file names, or email address, and are deleted after 30 days. Our server uses your IP address briefly to limit repeated requests and protect against abuse.

Messages you send us. If you use our contact or group pricing form, we receive what you enter (like your name, email, and message) and get an email notification so we can reply.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't use your uploaded documents or planner content to train AI models.
  • We don't run third-party advertising or ad trackers inside the planner.
  • We don't share your progress, notes, or absences with classmates, group members, or club officers.
  • We don't track grades or GPA.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers required to run Semester HQ, each receiving only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, and file storage
  • Cloudflare: our backend server, which handles AI requests, payments, forms, and fetching any calendar feed you connect
  • GitHub: hosting for the website and app files
  • Anthropic: reading documents, photos, and text when you use an AI feature
  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Resend and Google Workspace: emailing us your form submissions and handling our email
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, if we turn it on for our forms: a bot check that sees your IP address and browser details, never what you type

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Our marketing website (semester-hq.com, not the planner app) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate traffic. It is cookieless, and the site itself sets no cookies. The site also records anonymous counts of clicks on buttons like "Try the Demo" and "Get Started," with no cookies and no per-user identity. None of this is used for advertising, and none of it can see anything you do inside the planner.

Your controls

  • Export: Settings → Data → Export backup downloads your planner as a JSON file, any time.
  • Sharing: stop sharing a class, leave a study group or club, or delete your own messages and shared items at any time. Group owners and club officers can remove members and content in their groups.
  • Recently Deleted: items you delete are recoverable for 30 days before being permanently removed.
  • Erase this device: Settings → Data → Erase all data wipes the copy on that device.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account & Sync → Delete account cancels your subscription and permanently deletes your planner, notes, uploaded files, any leftover reminder data from earlier versions, and your sign-in. It doesn't automatically remove things you've shared with other people, like messages in a study group, a class you shared, or your membership in a club. Leave those or stop sharing first, or email us and we'll remove them for you. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.
  • Other requests: to access, correct, or delete your information, email us.

Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can see, correct, export and delete your data. Most of it you can do yourself: export from Settings → Data, correct anything inside the planner, and delete your account from Settings → Account & Sync. For anything else, email hello@semester-hq.com. We answer within 45 days, and we confirm it's you by asking you to write from the email on your account or to make the request while signed in. You may use an authorized agent; we'll ask for proof they act for you. We never treat you differently for exercising a right.

If you live in California

The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, gives you rights to know, delete and correct your personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. Here is how that maps to Semester HQ:

  • What we collect. Identifiers (name, email, account id); the content you create in the planner; commercial information (your subscription status); internet activity limited to crash reports and anonymous click counts; and, if a syllabus contains it, contact details for a professor or TA, which stays inside your account. We collect it from you, from Google when you sign in with Google, and from Stripe when you pay.
  • Why. To run the planner, sync it, bill you, fix bugs, prevent abuse, and answer you when you write to us.
  • Who receives it. Only the service providers listed above, under contracts that limit them to providing the service. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months. Because we don't, there is no "Do Not Sell or Share" setting to turn on, and browser Global Privacy Control signals change nothing about how we treat you.
  • Sensitive personal information. We don't collect it on purpose and don't use anything to infer characteristics about you. Your sign-in credentials are handled by Google Firebase, not stored by us.
  • Under 16. We don't sell or share anyone's personal information, including that of people under 16.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

  • Controller. Semester HQ, [legal entity name], [mailing address], California, USA. Contact: hello@semester-hq.com.
  • Legal bases. Performing our contract with you (your account, sync, billing, group plans); our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working (rate limiting, crash reports, abuse prevention); and your consent when you choose to send a document, photo or text to an AI feature, which you give each time by choosing to upload and can stop at any time. Connecting a calendar feed is consent of the same kind, given once when you paste the link; because it is then re-read while you use the app, you withdraw it by removing the feed in Settings.

Children's privacy

Semester HQ is intended for students aged 13 and older, and everyone confirms they're at least 13 when signing in. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Your planner is stored in Google Firebase with rules that let only your account read it. Payments never touch our servers. AI requests go through our own server so no key lives in your browser, and we cap how much any account can send. We test our security and fix what we find, but no service can promise perfect security, and you should keep your Google account or email secure since that is your key to Semester HQ.

Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies. The planner keeps a copy of your data and your sign-in session in your browser's own storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) so it opens instantly and works offline; deleting your account or signing out clears it. Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout and billing pages under Stripe's policy. If we ever add Cloudflare's bot check to our forms, it may set a cookie on Cloudflare's own challenge domain and nothing on ours.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and, for material changes, let you know in the app.
