Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is Semester HQ a subscription?

Yes. Semester HQ Plus is $7.99 a month, cancel anytime. You can try a live demo of the full interface first with no account, but Plus is what actually saves your semester, syncs it across devices, and unlocks syllabus upload, shared classes, study groups, and clubs and teams.

Do I need to pay to try it?

No. The live demo lets you click through every part of Semester HQ with no account and no payment, including a sample semester, study group, and club. It is a demo, though, not a saved account, so it resets when you close or reload the tab. Semester HQ Plus ($7.99 a month) is what actually keeps your semester, plus cross-device sync, syllabus upload, and everything you do with classmates.

Is my data private?

Before you sign in, nothing you enter is saved anywhere: it only exists in that browser tab and is gone once you leave. If you sign in, your planner data is stored in our database so you can reach it from other devices. We never sell your data or use it to train models.

What happens to a syllabus after I upload it?

It stays in your class page, so you can open it or re-read it later. It is yours until you delete the file or your account. To pull out your course, schedule, assignments, exam dates, office hours, and class policies, the file is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API. Anthropic does not use it to train models and does not keep it beyond its API data retention policy. What lands in your planner is fully editable, so you can fix anything it got wrong before it is final.

How many devices can I use it on?

Without an account, you can try Semester HQ in any browser, but nothing carries over between visits or devices. Signing in gives you one semester that is the same everywhere: laptop, phone, or a library computer, kept in sync.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from Settings, and you will keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then it stops automatically with no further charges. If you are within 14 days of your first charge and it is not for you, email us for a full refund instead.

What happens when the semester ends?

Nothing you have to think about. Your subscription is not tied to one semester: keep going every semester after for as long as you stay subscribed, or cancel anytime and pick back up later.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Semester HQ runs in your phone’s browser, no app store download needed. For the best experience, add it to your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. It opens like an app and works offline. Semester HQ never sends notifications, so nothing from it will ever show up on your lock screen.

Can my club, team, or chapter use it?

Yes. Start a club or team in Semester HQ and invite members with a code or link. Officers post meetings, practices, games, and dues deadlines, plus announcements, and every member gets them on their own calendar, with RSVPs. Group pricing covers the whole roster at a lower rate: $5.99 per member per month, self-serve for 5 to 50 seats.

What do classmates see when I share a class?

Only the class schedule: the course, class times, assignment and exam titles and due dates, and the office hours and policies from the syllabus. Your progress, notes, files, and absences are never shared.

Does it work with Canvas or my school’s LMS?

Yes, one direction: Semester HQ reads your due dates out of it. Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, and Moodle all give you a private calendar feed link, and pasting it in brings every deadline across, sorted into your classes. It stays connected, so when a professor moves a date, your copy moves the next time you open the app. Semester HQ does not write anything back to your LMS, and it is not affiliated with any of them. Importing a feed is part of Plus; without an account you can still upload an exported .ics file once.

Is there pricing for a whole class, department, or campus?

Yes. A group plan covers your club, team, chapter, or class section at $5.99 per member each month. An officer or instructor buys 5 to 50 seats at app.semester-hq.com/group-admin.html, shares one invite link, and changes the seat count whenever the roster does. For more than 50 seats, or for an invoice, PO, W-9, department, campus, or district, use the quote form on the group pricing page.

Email hello@semester-hq.com and a real person writes back. Or get started and see for yourself.
