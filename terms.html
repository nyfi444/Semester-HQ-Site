For more than 50 seats, or if you need an invoice, purchase order or W-9, or you're buying for a department, campus or district, ask for a quote on our group pricing page and we'll set the plan up with you. If you buy a group plan, you're the customer: you're responsible for the payment method, the charges, and how many seats you keep. Seats are billed monthly for the number you hold, and adding or removing seats is prorated by our payment processor.

  • Members get their own accounts. Anyone who takes a seat signs in as themselves and gets their own Semester HQ. They aren't charged while a seat covers them.
  • You choose who's covered. Anyone with the plan's invite link can take one of the seats you pay for, so share it only with the people you mean to cover. Plan admins can remove a member, which frees the seat and ends that member's paid features unless they subscribe themselves.
  • What an admin can see. A plan's admins see each member's name, email address, and the date they joined, so they know who they're paying for. They can't see any member's planner: classes, assignments, notes, files, and messages stay private to that member.
  • If the plan ends. Canceling, or a payment we can't collect, ends paid features for everyone on the plan at the end of the period you've paid for. Members keep their accounts and their own data, and can subscribe individually to turn paid features back on.

Connecting your LMS calendar

Semester HQ can read a calendar feed from Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle, or anything else that publishes one, so your due dates arrive without retyping. Three things are worth being clear about. It only reads: nothing is ever written back to your LMS, and nothing you do in Semester HQ changes your grades, submissions, or enrollment. The link is yours to give: connect one issued to you, and treat it as a password, since for most systems anyone holding it can read that calendar. And what arrives is only as good as what your school publishes. Feeds are often incomplete, sometimes wrong, and do not always carry a change your professor announced in class, so check the original before you rely on a date. Your school can change or withdraw its feeds at any time, and we have no control over that.
