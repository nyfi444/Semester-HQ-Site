Why Semester HQ exists

Built by a college student who was tired of managing one semester across six different apps.

Canvas had the assignments. A group chat had the actual deadlines. Notes were split across three different apps, and the syllabus PDFs were buried in Downloads. Semester HQ started as a way to put all of that in one place for one semester, and turned into something worth sharing with every other student living the exact same way.

College already asks enough of you. Keeping track of it shouldn’t be another class you’re failing.
