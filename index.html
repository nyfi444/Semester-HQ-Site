Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is this a subscription?

Semester HQ is $7.99/month to sign in and sync, cancel anytime. Using it locally on one device is free forever and never requires a subscription.

Do I need to pay to use it at all?

No. Semester HQ works fully in your browser with no account and no payment. Classes, assignments, notebook, and study timer all work for free, saved locally on your device. Signing in ($7.99/month) is only for the things that need an account: cross-device sync, AI syllabus upload, and shared study groups.

Is my data private?

By default, everything stays in your browser's local storage; nothing is sent anywhere. If you sign in, your planner data syncs to our database so you can reach it from other devices. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models.

What happens to a syllabus after I upload it?

Your file is sent to Anthropic's AI, read once to pull out your course, schedule, assignments, and exam dates, then discarded. It isn't stored afterward and is never used to train any model. What lands in your planner is fully editable, so you can fix anything it got wrong before it's final.

How many devices can I use it on?

Free use is limited to the one browser you're using: it's saved locally, so it doesn't follow you. Signing in removes that limit entirely, so sign in on your laptop, phone, or a library computer and it's the same semester, kept in sync everywhere.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from Settings, and you'll keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then it stops automatically with no further charges. If you're within 14 days of your first charge and it's not for you, email us for a full refund instead.

What happens when the semester ends?

Nothing you have to think about. Your subscription isn't tied to one semester: keep going every semester after for as long as you stay subscribed, or cancel anytime and pick back up later.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Semester HQ runs in your phone's browser, no app download needed.

Do you offer pricing for a university, class, or student group?

Yes. If you're covering Semester HQ for a department, club, or student organization, email hello@semesterhq.com for group rates.
