Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is this a subscription?

No. Founding Access is a one-time $19 payment — pay once, keep it for every semester after, for as long as Semester HQ exists. No recurring charges, ever.

Do I need to pay to use it at all?

No. Semester HQ works fully in your browser with no account and no payment — classes, assignments, grades, notebook, and study timer all work for free, saved locally on your device. Founding Access is only for the things that need an account: cross-device sync, AI syllabus upload, and shared study groups.

Is my data private?

By default, everything stays in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere. If you sign in, your planner data syncs to our database so you can reach it from other devices. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models.

What happens to a syllabus after I upload it?

Your file is sent to Anthropic's AI, read once to pull out your course, schedule, assignments, and exam dates, then discarded — it isn't stored afterward and is never used to train any model. What lands in your planner is fully editable, so you can fix anything it got wrong before it's final.

How many devices can I use it on?

Free use is limited to the one browser you're using — it's saved locally, so it doesn't follow you. Founding Access removes that limit entirely: sign in on your laptop, phone, or a library computer and it's the same semester, kept in sync everywhere.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

There's nothing to cancel — Founding Access is a one-time payment, not a subscription. If it's not for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.

What happens when the semester ends?

Nothing you have to think about. Founding Access isn't tied to one semester — start a new one anytime and keep using everything you already paid for, with no extra charge.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — Semester HQ runs in your phone's browser, no app download needed.
