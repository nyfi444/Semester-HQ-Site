Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Is this a subscription?

Yes. Semester HQ Plus is $7.99/month, cancel anytime. You can try the full interface for free with no account, but Plus is what actually saves your semester, syncs it across devices, and unlocks syllabus upload and study groups.

Do I need to pay to use it at all?

You can try every part of Semester HQ for free, with no account and no payment: classes, assignments, notebook, study timer, all of it. Without signing in, though, nothing is saved, so it disappears when you close or reload the tab. Semester HQ Plus ($7.99/month) is what actually keeps your semester, plus cross-device sync, syllabus upload, and shared study groups.

Is my data private?

Before you sign in, nothing you enter is saved anywhere: it only exists in that browser tab and is gone once you leave. If you sign in, your planner data is stored in our database so you can reach it from other devices. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models.

What happens to a syllabus after I upload it?

Your file is sent to Anthropic's AI, read once to pull out your course, schedule, assignments, and exam dates, then discarded. It isn't stored afterward and is never used to train any model. What lands in your planner is fully editable, so you can fix anything it got wrong before it's final.

How many devices can I use it on?

Without an account, you can try Semester HQ in any browser, but nothing carries over between visits or devices. Signing in gives you one semester that's the same everywhere: laptop, phone, or a library computer, kept in sync.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from Settings, and you'll keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then it stops automatically with no further charges. If you're within 14 days of your first charge and it's not for you, email us for a full refund instead.

What happens when the semester ends?

Nothing you have to think about. Your subscription isn't tied to one semester: keep going every semester after for as long as you stay subscribed, or cancel anytime and pick back up later.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Semester HQ runs in your phone's browser, no app download needed.

Do you offer pricing for a university, class, or student group?

Yes. If you're covering Semester HQ for a department, club, or student organization, tell us about your group and we'll send over rates.
