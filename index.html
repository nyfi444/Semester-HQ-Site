Questions, answered.

All questions
Does it work with Canvas or my school's LMS?

Yes, one direction: Semester HQ reads your due dates out of it. Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, and Moodle all give you a private calendar feed link, and pasting it in brings every deadline across, sorted into your classes. It stays connected, so when a professor moves a date, your copy moves the next time you open the app. Semester HQ does not write anything back to your LMS, and it is not affiliated with any of them.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Semester HQ runs in your phone's browser, no app store download needed. For the best experience, add it to your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. It opens like an app and works offline. Semester HQ never sends notifications, so nothing from it will ever show up on your lock screen.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from Settings, and you will keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then it stops automatically with no further charges. If you are within 14 days of your first charge and it is not for you, email us for a full refund instead.
