Questions

About Semester HQ as a college planner.

How is this different from Canvas or my school's LMS?

Canvas is run by your school and only covers what your professors put in it. Semester HQ is yours: it also holds your own notes, personal to-dos, study sessions, and anything from classes or systems Canvas doesn't touch.

Do I need a school email to use it?

No. Sign in with any Google account or any email address at all. No school affiliation or campus login is required.

Is it actually free?

The full interface is free to try with no account: calendar, assignments, notebook, study timer, exam tracker, project hub, and flashcards. Semester HQ Plus is $7.99/month for cross-device sync, syllabus upload, and study groups.
