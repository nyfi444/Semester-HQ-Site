Home screen

Put Semester HQ on your home screen.

Semester HQ is a web app at app.semester-hq.com, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen from its own icon, with the same account and the same semester on every device. It takes a few taps.

Get started → Try the live demo

What adding it gives you

  • It opens full screen from its own icon, without the browser’s address bar.
  • It is the same account and the same semester you see on every other device, kept in sync.
  • It opens without a connection and shows your semester as it last synced. Anything you change offline is saved on the device and syncs when you are back online.
  • On Android, press and hold the icon for two shortcuts: Quick capture and Start a focus session.

Adding it to your home screen does not turn on notifications. Semester HQ sends none. What is coming up is in the Heads up panel whenever you open the app.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open app.semester-hq.com in Safari

    Any page of the app works.

  2. Tap the Share button

    It is at the bottom of Safari on iPhone, and at the top on iPad.

  3. Tap Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list of options to find it.

  4. Tap Add

    The Semester HQ icon appears on your home screen. Open it from there from now on. If it asks you to log in once, do. Your semester is already saved to your account.

Using Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on an iPhone? Tap the Share button in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open app.semester-hq.com in Chrome

    Any page of the app works.

  2. Tap the three-dot menu

    It is at the top right of Chrome.

  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen

    The wording depends on your phone and your version of Chrome.

  4. Confirm

    Tap Install or Add. Semester HQ shows up with your other apps.

After you have added a class and come back a second time, Semester HQ may show its own Install button on the dashboard. Tap it to skip the menu. You can also find the steps any time in Settings, and at the end of semester setup.

On a laptop or desktop

In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon at the right end of the address bar and click it. Semester HQ then opens in its own window from your dock or taskbar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File, then Add to Dock. In any other browser, bookmark it with ⌘D on a Mac or Ctrl+D on Windows.

Questions about adding it to your home screen

Is there an app store download?

No. Semester HQ runs in your browser at app.semester-hq.com. Adding it to your home screen gives you the icon and the full screen view without a download.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Once you have opened it, the app loads without a connection and shows your semester as it last synced. Changes you make offline are saved on the device and sync when you are back online.

Will it send me notifications?

No. Semester HQ sends no notifications, installed or not. The Heads up panel lists what is coming up whenever you open the app.

Do I have to set anything up again?

No. It is the same account. If the home screen app asks you to log in once, do, and your classes, deadlines, and notes are already there.
