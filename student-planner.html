Questions

About the student planner.

Is this only for four-year college students?

No. Anyone taking courses with due dates and a schedule can use it: four-year college, community college, or grad school. There's no university affiliation required.

Does it work for grad school?

Yes. Courses, requirement types, and workload adapt to whatever your program looks like, not a template built only for a four-year undergrad schedule.

What if I switch majors or add a minor?

Nothing about the planner assumes a fixed major. Add, edit, or archive courses and semesters at any point as your program changes.
