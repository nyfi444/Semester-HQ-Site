Before you pay

The questions worth asking.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. The live demo is the real app with a sample semester, study group and club loaded, and it needs no account. Nothing in it is saved, and it resets when the page reloads. Plus is what actually keeps your semester and syncs it across devices.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from Settings, and your access runs to the end of the period you already paid for. If you email within 14 days of your first charge, you get that charge back in full.

What happens when the semester ends?

Nothing disappears. Past semesters stay in your account, and you start the next one fresh while keeping everything behind it.

Is there pricing for a whole class, department, or campus?

Groups are $5.99 per member per month, and an officer can set up 5 to 50 seats without talking to anyone. Past 50 seats, or if you need an invoice, a PO, or a W-9, ask for a quote.

Is my data private?

Your planner data is never sold and never used to train models. A syllabus you upload is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to be read, and nothing more. The full policy is short.
