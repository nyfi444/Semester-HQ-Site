Built for how classes actually assign work

A generic to-do app treats every task the same. College assignments aren't the same: a reading due before class isn't a problem set worth 15% of your grade, and a midterm needs a different kind of tracking than either. Semester HQ ties every assignment to its course, its due date and time down to 11:59 PM, and its point value, so the list you see actually reflects what each one is worth.

Add one and it lands on your calendar automatically, no separate step to keep a to-do list and a calendar in sync with each other. Exams get their own tracker with a live countdown. Bigger projects break into milestones with their own due dates instead of sitting as one deadline you ignore until the week before.

About the assignment tracker.

Does it sync to my calendar automatically?

Yes. Add an assignment and it lands on your calendar the same moment, color-coded by class, no separate step to keep the two in sync.

Can it track exams too, not just assignments?

Yes, exams get their own tracker with a live countdown, ranked by how soon each one is coming, pulled straight from your syllabus if you upload one.

What about group projects with multiple due dates?

Project Hub breaks a project into milestones with their own due dates, so a semester-long assignment doesn't sit as one looming deadline until the week before.
